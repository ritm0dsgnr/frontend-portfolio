Family house

Family house

London, 2021-2022

NM Art & Design designed the home and garden for temporary family living.

The architecture was created by the Marcel Wolterink Design Studio in Laren, Netherlands. The house includes five bedrooms, a living and dining room, a kitchen, a spa area, and two home offices. It also features a spacious terrace for outdoor living.

Inspired by the surrounding forest, the design incorporates floor-to — ceiling and ceiling-height windows that blur the line between indoor and outdoor spaces. The natural oak paneling, neutral colour palette, and linen textiles reflect a minimalist philosophy.
A highlight of this project is the collection of international contemporary art and collectible design objects, which add character and sophistication to the space.
