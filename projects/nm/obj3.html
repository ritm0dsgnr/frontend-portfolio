About Artist

Marina Beizel

Marina Beizel is a product designer who draws inspiration from the landscapes and architecture of our north, creating objects that are strongly associated with Russian identity. In her works, the author often reflects on the potential relationship between humans and their environment, as well as the tendency to forget the past in the whirlwind of today's events.
